# What is a quote?

A quote is a passage from your data that you want to keep at hand, usually because you plan to use it in your write-up. Think of it as highlighting the lines in an interview that you know you will want to cite later.

You can quote:
- Text in a document, a note, or a transcript
- Part of a PDF or an image
- Part of a respondent's answer in a survey

Every quote keeps a link to where it came from. Your quotes are listed in the Saved Quotes Panel, grouped by the document they were taken from. Click a quote there to go to it in its document.

You can use your quotes to:
- Copy them into your write-up. A quote from text is copied as text, and a quote from a PDF or an image is copied as a picture;
- Add them to a [Report](/guide/reports); and,
- Place them on a [relationship map](/guide/relationships), next to the codes and memos they relate to.

A quote is not the same as a code. A code marks every passage about a topic, so that you can analyse them together. A quote marks one passage that you want to use. You can quote a passage whether or not it is coded.

To create a quote, see [Creating quotes](/guide/creatingquotes).
